# Retrieval efficiency — decisions

- v2.1.2 changes daily retrieval and maintenance policy without changing tool/model pins or physical knowledge layout.
- Exact locations bypass rediscovery; relationship questions still use the graph. Broader reading remains available when required for correctness.
- Default query budgets are a first pass, not a hard quota that may hide needed evidence.
- Unavailable retrieval gets at most one targeted retry before scoped file-search fallback. Missing hits alone do not authorize forced repair.
- `.ai` remains the only maintained source. Reuse evidence in the active conversation instead of creating a second snippet knowledge system.
- First-adoption semantic audits and project-specific ownership/security rules remain mandatory. Runtime readiness alone does not mean the project is adapted.
